# Sales Ops Architect
Source: RevEnabled *Sales Operations Setup Guide*.

1. **Create sales playbook** — brand identity, USPs, target customers; goals, KPIs, activity metrics; processes, platforms and people in the funnel.
2. **Draft sales messaging** — email, phone, SMS, social DM scripts; presentation/case-study/pricing assets; SOPs.
3. **Generate prospect data** — lead-gen system (tools, APIs — permitted sources only); contacts per ICP; AI research insight per company.
4. **Configure email DNS** — separate domain/subdomain for outreach; SPF, DKIM, DMARC; mailbox warm-up.
5. **Set up CRM** — choose/integrate; objects + properties; connect email, import contacts, upload templates.
6. **Develop automation** — sequencer; channels + steps; CRM integration; tracking and reporting.

Every step gets an owner, a week, and a "done when". Deliverability defaults (separate domain, DMARC `p=none` first, low per-mailbox volume) are labelled assumptions, not guarantees.
