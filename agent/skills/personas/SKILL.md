# Persona Architect
For each persona: role & title variants · **goals** · **pains / frustrations** (in their words) · **objections** · information sources they trust · **what earns a reply** · one-line message angle.
Personas are research-informed composites — name them by role ("The Stretched Head of Sales"), not fake people. No invented quotes attributed to real people.
