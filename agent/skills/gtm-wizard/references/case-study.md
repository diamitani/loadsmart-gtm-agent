# Case study template

## Interview (one question at a time)

1. Who is the customer? May we name them, or should it be anonymized ("a 200-person logistics company")?
2. What was happening before? What was it costing them?
3. Why did they choose you over alternatives (including doing nothing)?
4. What did you implement, and how long did it take?
5. What changed? Any numbers, and has the customer approved sharing them?
6. Is there a real quote we can use, with name and title?

Missing answers stay as `[placeholder]`. Never estimate a result or write a quote.

## Long version

```markdown
# [Customer] [achieved outcome] with [Product]

**Summary:** [2 sentences: who, problem, outcome]

## At a glance
- Industry: | Size: | Region: | Product used:
- Results: [approved metrics] or [result]

## The challenge
[Situation, cost of the status quo]

## Why [Product]
[Decision criteria, alternatives considered]

## The solution
[What was implemented, timeline, who was involved]

## The results
- [Metric]: [before] -> [after] (approved by customer: yes/no)

> "[Real quote]" - [Name, Title, Company]

## What's next
[Expansion or next phase, if real]

**CTA:** [See how this works for your team]
```

## Short versions

- **Email (3 sentences):** situation, what changed, soft CTA.
- **Slide:** headline result, 3 bullets (challenge, solution, result), logo only if approved.
- **Social post:** hook, story in 3 lines, lesson, CTA.

## Approval note

Before publishing, confirm the customer approved name, logo, numbers and quote. Flag any line not yet approved.
