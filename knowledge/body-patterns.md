# Body patterns (60 to 90 words; swap in only brief facts)

**Signal, pain, product, question (default)**
> [Verified signal]. When [situation], [pain] usually [consequence]. [Product] [one-line value]. [Approved proof or omit]. [One question]?

**Role-first (when the signal is a leadership change)**
> Congrats on [role/move, if public]. In the first [period] teams in that seat often look at [pain]. [Product] [value]. [One question]?

**Peer question (no proof available)**
> [Verified signal]. Curious how your team handles [specific task] today. [Product] [value in one line]. Is that handled by [persona-owned role] or someone else?

**Day 4 new angle**
> One more angle for [Company]: [second verified point or persona-specific cost]. I can show it on a day of your own [freight type] if useful. Who owns [area]?

## Product value lines (company-stated, no numbers)

- Opendock: one place for dock, gate and yard scheduling.
- ShipperGuide: planning, procurement and execution of freight in one TMS.
- Loadsmart AI: agents finish repetitive freight tasks inside the systems you already run; Freight Experts handle exceptions.

## Never

Invented stats, fake urgency, fake familiarity, "per my last email", unverified competitor claims.
