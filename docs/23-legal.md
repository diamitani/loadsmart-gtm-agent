# 23. Legal, Privacy & Compliance Guidelines

## 1. Outbound Email Compliance Checklist
1. **Physical Address:** Every outbound sequence includes Loadsmart's registered Chicago headquarters address in the footer.
2. **Clear Opt-Out:** Every email contains a clear, 1-click unsubscribe or straightforward opt-out statement (*"If you are not the right person for logistics operations at {{Company}}, reply 'remove' and I will not contact you again"*).
3. **Subject Line Integrity:** Subject lines must accurately describe the email content without misleading deceptive prefixes (e.g. no fake `Re:` or `Fwd:`).
4. **Canadian Contacts (CASL):** Cold outreach to `.ca` domains or Canadian residents is disabled by default until explicit B2B referral or prior business relationship is documented.

## 2. Intellectual Property & AI Claims
- All claims generated by the AI must reference public company statements or Loadsmart verified marketing materials.
- No confidential competitor information or unauthorized trade secrets are ingested or cited.
